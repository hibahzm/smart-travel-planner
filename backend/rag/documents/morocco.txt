## Morocco — Destination Guide

Morocco is Africa's most visited country, an extraordinary mix of Berber, Arab, and French cultures straddling the Sahara Desert and the Atlas Mountains. The medinas (old cities) of Marrakech, Fes, and Chefchaouen are among the world's most atmospheric urban environments; the Sahara dunes at Merzouga are genuinely unmissable; and the coastal cities of Essaouira and Agadir offer Atlantic-facing beaches.

## Overview

Morocco covers 710,850 km². Key destinations:
- **Marrakech:** The Red City; the Jemaa el-Fna square is a UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage.
- **Fes:** The world's oldest university city, the largest car-free medieval city on earth.
- **Chefchaouen:** The Blue City of the Rif Mountains.
- **Sahara (Merzouga/Erg Chebbi):** Camel trekking into the dunes, desert camps, stargazing.
- **Atlas Mountains:** Trekking from Imlil to Toubkal (4,167m, highest peak in North Africa).
- **Essaouira:** Wind-swept Atlantic port city, music festival (Gnaoua World Music Festival, June).

## Best Time to Visit

**March–May and September–November:** Ideal. Temperatures 20–30°C in the south, 15–25°C in the north. Spring brings wildflowers in the Atlas. Autumn is the best time for Sahara (cool enough for comfortable camel trekking).

**Summer (June–August):** Very hot in Marrakech (40–45°C) and the south. The coast (Essaouira, Tangier) stays cool due to Atlantic trade winds. Ramadan (variable dates; check annually) significantly affects restaurant opening hours and some services.

**Winter (December–February):** Snow in the Atlas Mountains; excellent skiing at Oukaimeden (60 km from Marrakech, $15/day lift pass). Marrakech in winter is pleasant at 15–20°C.

## Getting In

Casablanca Mohammed V Airport (CMN) and Marrakech Menara Airport (RAK) handle the most European traffic. Royal Air Maroc, easyJet, Ryanair, and Transavia connect from major European hubs from ~$50–150 return. From Marrakech airport to the medina: petit taxi, ~60 MAD (~$6). Agree the price before entering; meters are often "broken."

## Getting Around

**Trains (ONCF):** Excellent and cheap. Casablanca–Marrakech: 3 hours, ~$12. Casablanca–Fes: 4.5 hours, ~$14. Trains are air-conditioned and reliable.

**CTM/Supratours buses:** Cover routes trains don't reach (Chefchaouen, Zagora, Essaouira). Book in advance in peak season.

**Grand taxis (shared):** Long-distance shared taxis fill with 6 passengers before departing. Cheaper than buses for some routes; no fixed schedule.

**Rental car:** Useful for the Atlas Mountains and desert route. International brands available from ~$25/day. Driving in medinas is inadvisable; park outside and walk.

## Costs

Morocco is excellent value. Budget: $25–40/day. Mid-range: $60–100/day. Luxury riads in Marrakech: $150–500/night.

- Mint tea (the national drink): 10–20 MAD ($1–2) at a café, free at riad check-in
- Street food (msemen flatbread, harira soup, brochettes): 20–50 MAD ($2–5)
- Mid-range restaurant meal: 80–150 MAD ($8–15)
- Hammam (traditional bath): 80–150 MAD ($8–15) for a full gommage scrub
- Riad in Marrakech medina: 400–800 MAD/night ($40–80) mid-range; 1,500+ MAD ($150+) high-end

## Top Experiences

**Marrakech Jemaa el-Fna:** The square transforms from fruit stalls by day to a medieval circus by night — snake charmers, Gnaoua musicians, storytellers, and hundreds of food stalls serving tagine, merguez, and camel brochettes. Eat at stall 1 or 14 (consistently praised); get the harira soup (5 MAD) from the stalls on the north side.

**Marrakech Medina:** Get lost in the souks intentionally. Dyers' souk (the most photogenic, behind Mouassine Mosque), spice souk, leatherwork souk. Bargain hard — opening price is 3–5× the accepted price. Saying "la shukran" (no thank you) and walking away is effective.

**Fes el-Bali:** The world's largest car-free urban area. The Bou Inania Madrasa, the Al-Attarine Souks, and the Chouara tannery (view from the surrounding leather shops — free if you buy something) are the highlights. A guide is strongly recommended for first-time visitors; the medina has 9,000 streets.

**Sahara Desert (Merzouga):** Drive or take a CTM bus from Marrakech (9 hours, $25) to the dunes. A night's camel trek into the dunes with a desert camp (dinner, stargazing, breakfast, morning return): $50–100. The sunrises at Erg Chebbi are extraordinary.

**Toubkal Trek:** Africa's second-highest peak can be summitted in 2 days from Imlil (day 1 to Refuge du Toubkal, day 2 summit and descend). No technical equipment needed June–October. Cost: guide mandatory from Imlil ($50/day), mule hire optional ($25/day).

## Practical Tips

- Dirham (MAD) is not freely convertible outside Morocco — exchange or withdraw on arrival. ATMs are plentiful in cities.
- Women travelling solo: harassment is common in medinas. Confident, direct "no" works. Covering shoulders and knees reduces it significantly. Wearing a ring (married) helps.
- Photography: always ask before photographing people. In souks, shopkeepers sometimes demand payment for photos of their goods.
- Booking riads: go direct or via booking.com — "travel agencies" outside medina gates take 30–50% commission.
- Tipping: 10% in restaurants (not expected in street stalls), small tip for hammam attendants.
