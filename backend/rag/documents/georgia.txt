## Georgia (Country) — Destination Guide

Georgia is the Caucasus's most surprising destination: a country with 8,000 years of wine-making history, a unique script and language, ancient cave cities and cliff monasteries, and a capital (Tbilisi) with one of the world's most atmospheric old quarters. It is extraordinarily affordable, the food is exceptional, the hospitality is legendary, and it sees a fraction of the tourism of comparable destinations.

## Overview

Georgia sits between Russia to the north, Turkey and Armenia to the south, and Azerbaijan to the east. Key destinations:
- **Tbilisi:** The ancient capital on the Mtkvari River; sulphur baths, the Old Town (Abanotubani), wine bars, and contemporary art.
- **Kazbegi (Stepantsminda):** The Caucasus Mountains; Gergeti Trinity Church at 2,170m with Mount Kazbek (5,047m) behind it.
- **Kakheti (wine region):** Georgia's wine country; qvevri (clay pot) wine, ancient monasteries (Bodhi, Alaverdi), and the Alazani Valley.
- **Svaneti:** Remote mountain region, medieval tower villages, glaciers.
- **Batumi:** Black Sea coast, subtropical climate, modern architecture.
- **Vardzia:** 12th-century cave monastery complex carved into a volcanic cliff.

## Best Time to Visit

**May–June and September–October:** Peak hiking and wine harvest season. The Caucasus Mountains are passable, Kazbegi road is open, and Tbilisi is pleasant at 20–28°C. The Rtveli (grape harvest, September–October) in Kakheti is one of the country's great experiences — stay at a family winery.

**July–August:** Hot in Tbilisi (35–38°C); excellent in the mountains and on the Black Sea coast. The Kazbegi highland is at its most beautiful.

**Winter:** Tbilisi stays mild (5–10°C); Gudauri ski resort (2,196m) has reliable snow November–April, lift passes $25–35/day — exceptional value.

## Getting In

Tbilisi International Airport (TBS). Turkish Airlines, Wizz Air, Ryanair, Pegasus, and FlyDubai serve TBS from European and Middle Eastern hubs. Fares from Istanbul: $30–80 return. From London via Istanbul: $200–350 return. No direct flights from North America; connect via Istanbul, Dubai, or Vienna.

## Getting Around

**Marshrutky (shared minibuses):** The default intercity transport. Tbilisi–Kazbegi: 2.5 hours, 10 GEL ($3.70). Tbilisi–Signagi (Kakheti): 2 hours, 15 GEL ($5.50). No booking needed; departs when full.

**Taxis:** Bolt app works in Tbilisi and Batumi. Metered Tbilisi fares: 5–15 GEL ($1.85–5.50) for city trips.

**Car rental:** Recommended for Kakheti wine routes, Svaneti, and Vardzia. Budget Georgian rentals from $25–40/day. Roads outside Tbilisi range from excellent (Military Highway to Kazbegi) to rough gravel (Svaneti). The Svaneti road requires care in wet weather.

## Costs

Georgia is Western Europe's best-kept travel secret for value. Budget: $25–40/day. Mid-range: $50–80/day. Luxury (Rooms Hotel Tbilisi, Ambassadori Tbilisi): $150–300/night.

- Khachapuri (cheese bread): 8–15 GEL ($3–5.50) at a local bakery
- Khinkali (soup dumplings, 8 pieces): 10–18 GEL ($3.70–6.60)
- Glass of house wine in a wine bar: 5–15 GEL ($1.85–5.50)
- Sit-down restaurant meal with wine: 40–80 GEL ($15–30) per person
- Sulphur bath (private cabin, 1 hour): 30–60 GEL ($11–22) per person
- Guesthouse (gomi) in Kazbegi: 60–100 GEL ($22–37)/night including breakfast
- Mid-range hotel Tbilisi: 150–250 GEL ($55–93)/night

## Top Experiences

**Tbilisi Old Town:** The sulphur bath district (Abanotubani) has operated since the 5th century; domed brick bath houses rise from the cliff behind the waterfall. A private sulphur bath cabin for two: GEL 60–100/hour. The Narikala Fortress above has free entry and the city's best views. Rustaveli Avenue is the cultural spine; the National Museum (Rustaveli 3) has a world-class collection of Georgian gold for GEL 15 ($5.50).

**Gergeti Trinity Church (Kazbegi):** The 14th-century church perched on a ridge at 2,170m with Mount Kazbek (5,047m) behind it is one of the Caucasus's defining images. Walk up from Stepantsminda village (2.5 hours each way) or hire a 4WD taxi (GEL 50–80 return). A clear morning — clouds gather by noon. The 2-day Kazbek summit trek requires experience, guides, and acclimatisation.

**Kakheti Wine Region:** Georgia's qvevri (clay pot) wine-making tradition is UNESCO-listed. Alazani Valley family wineries offer free tastings with purchase; Pheasant's Tears winery in Signagi is the most acclaimed natural wine producer. The Bodhi Monastery (11th century) and Alaverdi Cathedral are the architectural highlights. Stay at a family guesthouse (GEL 100–150/night including meals and wine).

**Svaneti:** The remote mountain province with its medieval stone towers is the most dramatic landscape in the Caucasus. The journey from Tbilisi is 7–8 hours (marshrutka, GEL 35). Mestia is the main town; the 2-day Mestia-to-Ushguli trek through Svan villages is world-class. Ushguli (2,200m) is the highest continuously inhabited village in Europe.

**Vardzia Cave Monastery:** A 13th-century monastery city carved into a volcanic cliff, with frescoed halls, wine cellars, and a spring that still flows. 3-hour drive from Tbilisi via Borjomi spa town. Entry: GEL 7 ($2.60).

## Practical Tips

- Georgian Lari (GEL): 1 USD ≈ 2.70 GEL. ATMs in Tbilisi accept all cards; smaller towns prefer cash.
- Georgian hospitality (tamada): a traditional feast (supra) with a toastmaster is one of the great cultural experiences. If invited to a local home, bring wine or sweets; the host will be offended if you hold back.
- Language: Georgian script (Mkhedruli) looks like nothing else. Download a Georgian keyboard for Google Translate camera mode — essential for menus and signs outside Tbilisi.
- Safety: Georgia is very safe; petty crime is rare even at night in Tbilisi.
- Hiking in the Caucasus: Weather changes rapidly; always carry waterproofs and extra layers above 2,000m.
